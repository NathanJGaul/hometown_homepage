Top Three Activities to Do in Boise

Boise Greenbelt

Walk, bike, or kayak along this scenic 25-mile path that winds through parks, wildlife areas, and downtown Boise.

Downtown Farmers Market

Experience local culture with fresh produce, artisan goods, and vibrant street life in Boise’s bustling downtown.

Hiking

Discover stunning views and outdoor adventure on miles of trails like the iconic Camel’s Back Park or Table Rock.
